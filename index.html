About

Hi! My name is Amelia Overstreet and I’m a junior studying Comparative Literature and Anthropology. I’m from Sonoma County, CA, and I live in Columbae on campus!

Interests

Academically

I’m interested in the power of stories and storytelling, particularly in postcolonial or decolonial settings, interdisciplinary & qualitative research, American Jewry, and the intersection between religion and politics.

Outside of class

I spend lots of time thinking about community building, and am super passionate about cooperative living, peer-facilitated spaces and events, and education reform, accessibility, and curriculum development. You can find me planning events and making coffees at OnCall Cafe, as a peer-counselor at SHPRC, or on the Columbae porch. I also (still) spend lots of time reading.

Project interests POLISCI 158: AI & Politics

In this course, I think I’d like to study the intersection of AI and education, namely, how AI is being incorporated into school settings, and how this incorporation is affecting school-kids. I’m considering questions such as:

  • How can AI use policies be more effective in the classroom?
  • Are there cases of grade schools in America that are implementing AI in supplementary ways?
  • Which school-aged populations have been most affected by learning loss from AI use in middle and high school?
